# ShopSphere Prototype Walkthrough (Short Version)

**[Introduction]**
"Welcome to ShopSphere, our unified platform connecting local shopkeepers, customers, and system administrators. Let’s quickly dive into the three main portals of our prototype."

**[The Shopkeeper Portal]**
"First, the **Shopkeeper Portal**, designed to simplify daily business operations:
* The **Dashboard** gives a quick snapshot of total sales and low stock alerts.
* **Inventory** is where shopkeepers manage and track their stock in real-time.
* **Sales Tracking** provides a clear history of every transaction.
* A key feature is the **Credit Book**, a digital 'Udhaar' ledger to track customer dues and partial payments.
* **Combo Offers** lets shopkeepers bundle items together for discounts, driving more sales.
* **Analytics** visualizes revenue trends and top-selling items to help make smart business decisions.
* Finally, the **Shop Network** helps shops discover B2B collaborations, and the **Profile** tab handles store details."

**[The Customer Portal]**
"Next is the **Customer Portal**, which makes local shopping incredibly convenient:
* The **Local Market** tab lets customers search for specific products and instantly see which nearby shops have them in stock.
* **Browse Shops** gives a directory view of the neighborhood, showing store hours and active combo offers.
* **My Profile** allows customers to view their purchase history and track their outstanding credit with local shops."

**[The Administration Portal]**
"Lastly, we have the **Administration Portal**, the backbone of the platform:
* The **Admin Overview** provides platform-wide metrics like total active shops and customers.
* **Manage Users** allows admins to onboard new shopkeepers and handle support.
* **System Inventory** gives a global view of product catalogs, helping standardize items across the platform.
* And **Utilities** contains the technical settings to keep everything running smoothly."

**[Conclusion]**
"In summary, ShopSphere modernizes local retail by giving shopkeepers better tools, giving customers easy local discovery, and giving admins complete oversight. Thanks for watching!"
